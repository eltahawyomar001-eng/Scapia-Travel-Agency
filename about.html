About SCAPIA

We're passionate about creating extraordinary travel experiences that transform the way you see the world.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, SCAPIA began with a simple mission: to make travel accessible, memorable, and transformative for everyone. What started as a small team of travel enthusiasts has grown into a global travel company serving thousands of adventurers each year.

We believe that travel has the power to change lives. It opens our minds, connects us with different cultures, and creates memories that last a lifetime. That's why we're dedicated to crafting journeys that go beyond the ordinary.
