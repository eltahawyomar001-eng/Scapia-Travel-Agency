Southeast Asia is filled with incredible destinations that most travelers overlook. While places like Bali and Bangkok are wonderful, there's a whole world of hidden gems waiting to be discovered.

## 1. Koh Rong Samloem, Cambodia

This small island off the coast of Cambodia offers pristine beaches without the crowds. Crystal-clear waters, bioluminescent plankton at night, and a truly unplugged experience make this a must-visit.

## 2. Ninh Binh, Vietnam

Often called "Halong Bay on land," this stunning landscape of limestone karsts and rice paddies is far less crowded than its famous maritime counterpart.

## 3. Siargao, Philippines

Before it becomes the next big thing, visit this teardrop-shaped island known for its surf breaks, lagoons, and laid-back atmosphere.

## 4. Luang Prabang, Laos

This UNESCO World Heritage town offers French colonial architecture, Buddhist temples, and the famous morning alms-giving ceremony.

## 5. Gili Air, Indonesia

The quietest of the Gili Islands, Gili Air offers the perfect balance of relaxation and activities without Gili T's party scene.
