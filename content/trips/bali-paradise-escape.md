Discover the enchanting island of Bali on this 7-day adventure. From ancient temples to pristine beaches, lush rice terraces to vibrant nightlife, this trip offers the perfect blend of culture, relaxation, and adventure.

Experience traditional Balinese ceremonies, explore underwater worlds while snorkeling, trek through scenic rice paddies, and indulge in world-class spa treatments. This is Bali at its finest.

## Highlights

- Sunrise trek to Mount Batur volcano
- Traditional Balinese cooking class
- Snorkeling in crystal-clear waters of Nusa Penida
- Visit ancient temples and sacred sites
- Explore the artistic town of Ubud
- Relax on stunning beaches of Seminyak
