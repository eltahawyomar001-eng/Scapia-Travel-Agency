Adventure Beyond The Edge

Patagonia, Argentina 7 Days

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the wilderness of Patagonia. From towering glaciers to pristine lakes, rugged mountains to vast steppes, this trip offers the perfect blend of adventure, natural beauty, and authentic cultural experiences.

Experience the raw power of nature as you trek through stunning landscapes, witness incredible wildlife, and connect with the spirit of one of the world's most remote and beautiful regions.

What's Included

  • 6 nights accommodation in boutique lodges
  • Daily breakfast and 3 gourmet dinners
  • Airport transfers and all transportation
  • Professional English-speaking guides
  • All entrance fees to national parks
  • Trekking equipment and safety gear

Gallery

Itinerary

1

Arrival in El Calafate

Welcome to Patagonia! Upon arrival at El Calafate Airport, you'll be greeted and transferred to your boutique lodge overlooking the lake.

2

Perito Moreno Glacier

Visit the magnificent Perito Moreno Glacier. Walk along the viewing platforms and witness massive chunks of ice calving into the lake.

3

Transfer to El Chaltén

Scenic drive to El Chaltén, the trekking capital of Argentina. Afternoon exploration of the charming mountain village.

4

Fitz Roy Trek

Full-day trek to Laguna de los Tres for stunning views of Mount Fitz Roy. One of the most iconic hikes in Patagonia.

5

Torre Glacier Hike

Hike to Cerro Torre base camp. Marvel at the jagged spire and the stunning glacier lake at its base.

6

Leisure Day

Free day to explore at your own pace. Optional activities include rock climbing, horseback riding, or relaxing at the spa.

7

Departure

Enjoy a farewell breakfast before your transfer to El Calafate Airport for your departure flight. ¡Hasta pronto!
